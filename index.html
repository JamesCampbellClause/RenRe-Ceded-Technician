The role

Ceded Technician, explained

You will own the ceded post bind side of the business for a defined ceded external portfolio and internal cessions written across RenRe's platforms. That means making sure ceded contract terms are complied with, analysing and processing ceded reinsurance contracts and collateral agreements, and supporting the financial accounting, internal controls and reporting that sit behind them. You will work closely with underwriters, brokers, finance and operations, and help improve how ceded processes run in an increasingly complex, global environment.
Day to day

What you will actually be doing

Contract set up and processing

  • Interpret and set up ceded reinsurance contracts and collateral trust agreements in REMS (underwriting) and SICS (accounting)
  • Review, analyse and process ceded transactions so final terms are complete and accurate
  • Keep contract management and electronic filing in good order

Close, controls and reporting

  • Support the ceded side of the monthly and quarterly close
  • Make sure SOX 404 processes exist and internal controls are followed
  • Reconcile ceded accruals and recoveries in SICS and support internal and external reporting

Bordereaux and recoveries

  • Prepare and analyse ceded proportional bordereaux and claims recovery notifications
  • Explain actual vs expected and gross to net variances to underwriters for sign off
  • Monitor large catastrophe movements and their impact on the ceded portfolio

Cash and collateral

  • Cash pairing in SICS and reinsurer cash reconciliations
  • Monitor receivables ageing, chase recoveries and settle payables when due
  • Process collateral amendments, withdrawals and releases, keeping unapplied cash to a minimum at quarter end

Stakeholders and the Lloyd's box

  • Proactively liaise with brokers, reinsurers, underwriters and other functions
  • Work with internal and external auditors
  • Provide underwriting support at the Lloyd's box

Projects and improvement

  • Help develop and document processes, controls, systems and solutions
  • Support local and global projects and workstreams
  • Back up colleagues across platforms and support senior team members

Jargon buster

Systems and terms you will meet

SICS

The reinsurance accounting system where ceded accruals, recoveries, cash pairing and collateral are processed and reconciled.

REMS

The underwriting system where ceded contract terms are set up alongside SICS.

Bordereaux

Periodic reports of premiums and claims under proportional treaties, analysed against expected figures.

SOX 404

Sarbanes Oxley requirements for documented, tested internal controls over financial reporting at US listed companies.

Collateral trust agreements

Arrangements where reinsurers post collateral to secure their obligations, which may need amending, withdrawing or releasing.

The Lloyd's box

The syndicate's underwriting desk in the Lloyd's building, where brokers meet underwriters face to face.
